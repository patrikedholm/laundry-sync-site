laundry-sync

A personal tool that keeps a Google Calendar in sync with laundry-room bookings.

laundry-sync periodically reads its owner’s upcoming bookings from the building’s laundry booking system and creates, updates and cancels one Google Calendar event per booking, with the reminders and event text the owner has configured.

It is a single-user application, run by and for its owner. It is not offered as a service to anyone else, and only the owner’s Google account can sign in to it.

The sync is one-way: the booking system is the source of truth, and nothing done in the calendar is written back to it.
